# cs330-portfolio
SNHU CS-330 Computational Graphics and Visualization portfolio artifacts.

How do I approach designing software?
I usually start by picturing what I want the final result to look like. For this project, I used one of my own real photos as the reference for my 3D scene. Breaking the idea down into smaller parts helped me stay organized and made the design feel more manageable.

What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?
This project helped me think more in 3D instead of just flat layouts. I learned how to plan object placement, lighting, and camera angles in a way that makes the whole scene feel balanced.

What design process did you follow for your project work?
My process was simple: start with the real photo, sketch out the layout, translate it into shapes and coordinates, and then adjust things as I tested the scene. I repeated this until everything looked right.

How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?
Breaking big ideas into smaller steps and using real references are tactics I can use in future classes or projects. It keeps things clear and helps avoid getting overwhelmed.

How do I approach developing programs?
I try to build things in layers. I add one object, test it, then move on to the next. This keeps the project stable and makes debugging easier.

What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?
I reused transformation logic, kept my shader setup clean, and made sure object positions were easy to tweak. This helped me avoid rewriting large chunks of code.

How did iteration factor into your development?
Iteration was a big part of the project. I would run the program, see what looked off, and then go back and adjust the code. Each small change made the scene look more like the real photo.

How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project’s completion?
At the start, I was just trying to get anything to render. By the end, I was thinking more about structure, organization, and how to make the code easier to maintain. My workflow became more consistent and planned out.

How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?
Computer science gives me the tools to build things from scratch and understand how they work. It helps me turn ideas into something visual, interactive, and useful.

How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future educational pathway?
Graphics taught me how 3D worlds are built, how lighting works, and how transformations affect what the user sees. These skills can help in future classes that deal with simulations or interactive environments.

How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future professional pathway?
Knowing how to work with visualizations can help in areas like UI/UX, game development, training tools, or any job where visual representation matters. It’s a skill that can be used in a lot of different fields.
